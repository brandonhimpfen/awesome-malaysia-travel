> A curated list of resources for traveling in Malaysia, covering official tourism boards, transport networks, regional guides, national parks, and digital nomad resources.

Malaysia is a culturally diverse Southeast Asian destination bridging the Malay Peninsula and the island of Borneo, renowned for its modern capital, ancient rainforests, and multicultural culinary heritage. This list is intended for international travelers, digital nomads, wildlife explorers, and cultural heritage researchers.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, immigration requirements, and weather services.

- [Tourism Malaysia (Official Portal)](https://www.malaysia.travel/) - Official national tourism board portal featuring destination highlights, event calendars, and cultural itineraries.
- [Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) Portal](https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main) - Official immigration pre-arrival registration system required for international visitors entering Malaysia.
- [Malaysia Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture (MOTAC)](https://www.motac.gov.my/) - Official government ministry regulating tourism standards, licensed tour operators, and heritage sites.
- [Malaysian Meteorological Department (MetMalaysia)](https://www.met.gov.my/) - Official national weather service providing monsoon forecasts, regional climate warnings, and radar tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating intercity rail networks, domestic aviation, rapid transit systems, and bus services.

- [KTMB (Keretapi Tanah Melayu Berhad)](https://www.ktmb.com.my/) - National railway operator managing the ETS intercity electric train service and KTM Komuter lines across Peninsular Malaysia.
- [Malaysia Airlines](https://www.malaysiaairlines.com/) - National flag carrier operating domestic and international flights connecting Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL) with regional hubs.
- [AirAsia](https://www.airasia.com/) - Major low-cost carrier operating extensive domestic and regional routes across Southeast Asia.
- [Rapid KL Public Transport](https://myrapid.com.my/) - Official transit operator for Kuala Lumpur's LRT, MRT, monorail, and bus networks using Touch 'n Go cards.

## Destinations & Regions

### Kuala Lumpur & Selangor

- [Visit Kuala Lumpur](https://www.visitkl.gov.my/) - Official municipal tourism portal for exploring the Petronas Twin Towers, historic Merdeka Square, and culinary night markets.
- [Batu Caves Official Guide](https://www.malaysia.travel/) - Visitor portal for exploring the iconic limestone caves, Hindu shrines, and giant golden Murugan statue just outside KL.

### Penang & Northern Region

- [Penang Global Tourism](https://mypenang.gov.my/) - Official regional portal for George Town's UNESCO heritage shophouses, street art trails, and world-renowned hawker food centers.
- [Langkawi Development Authority (LADA)](https://www.lada.gov.my/) - Official visitor guide for Langkawi's duty-free island beaches, geopark cable cars, and mangrove boat tours.

### Borneo (Sabah & Sarawak)

- [Sabah Tourism Board](https://www.sabahtourism.com/) - Official regional hub for climbing Mount Kinabalu, Sepilok orangutan rehabilitation, and Sipadan diving adventures.
- [Sarawak Tourism Board](https://sarawaktourism.com/) - Official travel guide for Kuching's cultural heritage, Bako National Park, and Mulu Caves UNESCO site.

### East Coast Islands & Highlands

- [Visit Pahang / Cameron Highlands](https://www.pahangtourism.com.my/) - Regional tourism guide for tea plantations, strawberry farms, and cool mountain hiking trails.
- [Perhentian & Redang Islands Guide](https://www.malaysia.travel/) - Visitor portal for crystal-clear coral diving and marine parks off the Terengganu coast.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Malaysia's DE Rantau nomad pass.

- [Malaysia Digital Nomad Pass (MDEC DE Rantau)](https://mdec.my/derantau/) - Official government digital nomad visa program offering long-term residency and coworking ecosystem benefits.
- [Komune Co-working Kuala Lumpur](https://www.komune.com.my/) - Popular community workspace and hub for remote professionals and startups in the Klang Valley.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contact numbers, medical support, and local laws.

- [Malaysia Emergency Hotline (999)](https://www.999.gov.my/) - Direct national emergency response dispatch for police, ambulance, and fire services.
- [Tourist Police Malaysia](https://www.malaysia.travel/) - Dedicated police assistance division supporting international visitors in major urban and tourist zones.
